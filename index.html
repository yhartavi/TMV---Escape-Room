🔐 Escape the Vault

You’ve been locked inside the bank’s vault. Ten time-locked doors stand between you and the exit — and each opens only with the right time-value-of-money answer. The clock is ticking… and as you know, time is money.

10 locksSolve each to move to the next room.
20 minutesWhen the clock hits zero, the vault seals.
3 attemptsEach wrong try costs 25 pts. Three misses jam the lock (0 pts).
HintsAvailable on every lock, but cost 30 pts.

Each lock is worth up to 100 points. Escape before time runs out to earn a time bonus (1 point for every 4 seconds left). Ties are broken by fastest time. Have a calculator ready — round money to 2 decimals and rates to 2 decimal places (e.g. 9.86 for 9.86%).
